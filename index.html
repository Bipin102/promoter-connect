How it works

If you're hiring

  1. Post the event. Date, venue, pay, how many people, and anything specific like dress code or gender.
  2. Choose normal or urgent. Urgent posts get a 60-minute window and sit at the top of the job list with a countdown.
  3. Watch the spots fill. Bookings confirm instantly, and two people can never take the same last spot.
  4. See who's arrived. Promoters check in from the venue with a photo, plus their location if they allow it.
  5. Close it out. Mark the event complete and rate each promoter.

If you're a promoter

  1. Set up your profile. Photo, city, languages, and the brands you've worked with.
  2. Find gigs. Filter by city, date, category or pay.
  3. Apply or book. Apply to normal posts. Urgent spots are first come, first served, so you can book them straight away.
  4. Check in at the venue. One photo when you get there, and the company knows you've arrived.
  5. Rate them back. After the event you rate the company too, including how the payment went.

Why we built it this way

Most promoter hiring still happens through WhatsApp groups and a long list of phone numbers. That works until you need six people by this evening. A few things we cared about:

"Urgent" means one hour

If the hour runs out before every spot is filled, the post says so. You can reopen it, edit it, or cancel. It won't pretend you're fully staffed.

Proof that people turned up

Check-in photos are separate from profile pictures and are taken at the venue, so you're not relying on a "reached" message.

Ratings go both ways

Companies rate promoters on punctuality and work. Promoters rate companies on how they were treated and paid. Badges come from those numbers, not from us.

Questions people ask

Is it only for last-minute hiring?

No. Normal posts work like a regular job listing: promoters apply and you pick who to confirm. Urgent is there for when you're short on the day.

What happens if nobody books in the hour?

The post is marked as "window ended" with however many people did book. You decide whether to reopen it for longer or cancel it.

How do I know a promoter is reliable?

Every profile shows how many events they've completed and their ratings from companies they've worked with. A rating can only be left after an event is marked complete.

Can I use one account as both a promoter and a company?

Each account is one or the other. If you need both, sign up a second time with a different email.
